# M5-4 Launch the curator at the threshold

## Why

Knowledge notes pile up unread unless something starts the curator. The model dispatcher did it when it remembered.

## What to build

- After a close, count raw notes; at the threshold, launch the curator (role `curator`, its own settings: writes only the knowledge folder), one at a time, with a time limit after which its terminal is ended and a notice sent.
- The threshold rule is ported, with the cases of its tests.

## Carried from

- claude-org-ja: `tools/check_curate_threshold.py`, `tools/test_check_curate_threshold.py`
- paddock: `templates/curator.md`, `skills/org-curate/`

## Definition of done

The first commit adds `tests/curator.rs`, failing, with the trailer `Acceptance-Test: tests/curator.rs`. At head `cargo test --test curator` passes and asserts:

- Below the threshold nothing is launched.
- At the threshold one curator is launched; a second close while it runs launches none.
- A curator past its time limit is ended and a notice queued.
