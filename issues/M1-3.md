# M1-3 Events and resync

## Why

claude-org-ja once lost a terminal's end because an old cursor outlived a restart (#1235).

## What to build

- `events()` (spec section 6.5): one `events.subscribe`; a missed-events notice, a heartbeat gap of 45 s or a reconnect yields a `Resync`.
- Reconnect with backoff (1 s to 30 s). Positions in the base's stream are never compared across connections.

## Definition of done

The first commit adds `tests/test_events_resync.py`, failing, with the trailer `Acceptance-Test: tests/test_events_resync.py`. At head it passes and asserts:

- Events for a scripted agent arrive in order.
- Restarting the base mid-test yields a `Resync`, and a terminal that finished during the gap is absent from its list.
- A dropped connection is re-established without the caller doing anything.
