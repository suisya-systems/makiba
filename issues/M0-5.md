# M0-5 Carry the state.db schema and writer, and add migration v4

## Replaces what was merged

Issue #7 was closed by pull request #58, which carried the schema, the writer and the queries in Python (`makiba/db/*.py`) and added migration v4. The owner decided on 2026-11-18 that makiba is implemented in Rust (plan.md section 5). This Issue is reopened and replaces that code: `schema.sql` and `schema_v4.sql` are kept and embedded; the Python is ported or dropped as listed, and removed.

## Why

The records are the part of claude-org-ja worth keeping unchanged: runs, events and the delivery ledger with its idempotency key.

## What to build

- `src/db/schema.sql` and `src/db/schema_v4.sql`: moved unchanged from `makiba/db/` and embedded in the binary with `include_str!`. They are the SQL makiba runs; the Rust code does not restate them.
- `src/db/mod.rs`: opens `state.db` with rusqlite (SQLite bundled into the binary) with the pragmas `makiba/db/__init__.py` sets, and brings a database to v4 as its `migrate` does (the carried schema, its in-place upgrades, then v4 in one transaction).
- `src/db/writer.rs` and `src/db/queries.rs`: the parts of `makiba/db/writer.py` and `queries.py` that makiba uses, ported: transactions; projects, runs and their status; worker folders; the session row; events; the delivery ledger with its idempotency key (pending deliveries, an attempt, delivered, failed); active runs, a run by task id, recent events. Their test cases, ported, in `tests/db_writer.rs` and `tests/db_queries.rs`.
- Remove the Python merged by #58: `makiba/db/*.py`, `tests/db/` and `tests/test_schema_v4.py`.
- Migration v4 is the tables of spec section 5 (`agents`, `commands`, `run_launch`, `messages`, `pending`, `pr_watches`, `dispatcher`), already in `schema_v4.sql`.
- The carried contract documents `docs/contracts/state-semantics-contract.md` and `delegation-lifecycle-contract.md` stay as merged.

## Carried from

- makiba itself (merged in #58): `makiba/db/schema.sql` and `schema_v4.sql` (kept as SQL), `writer.py`, `queries.py` and `tests/db/` (ported)
- claude-org-ja: `tools/state_db/schema.sql`, `writer.py`, `queries.py`, `test_writer.py`, `test_queries.py` (the sources of the above)
- claude-org-ja: `docs/contracts/state-semantics-contract.md`, `docs/contracts/delegation-lifecycle-contract.md`

## Not in this Issue

- `importer.py`, `migrate_workers.py`, `extract_freetext.py`, `curator_archive.py`: they import claude-org-ja's old files and are not needed.
- `snapshotter.py`, and the writer's snapshot regeneration and worker-file archive: they wrote claude-org-ja's `.state/org-state.md`, its JSON snapshot and its worker files, none of which makiba has; makiba's view of the records is `makiba status`.
- The resume-briefing and organization-summary helpers of `queries.py`: their readers, claude-org-ja's resume and dashboard, are not carried. M6-5 renews the secretary from the `pending` and `runs` tables.

## Definition of done

The first commit adds `tests/schema_v4.rs`, failing, with the trailer `Acceptance-Test: tests/schema_v4.rs`. At head `cargo test --test schema_v4` passes and asserts:

- A fresh database migrates to v4 and has every table and CHECK of spec section 5.
- A second `(source_event_id, recipient)` delivery row is refused.
- A `run_launch` row with an unknown `stage` is refused.
