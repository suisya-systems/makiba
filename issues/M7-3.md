# M7-3 Move the projects claude-org-ja serves

## Why

Until the projects claude-org-ja serves move, two organizations run side by side. paddock is not one of them: it continues as a lightweight organization beside makiba (plan.md section 4.1), and the projects it serves stay with it.

## What to build

- Register each project claude-org-ja serves; move their goal ledgers; stop claude-org-ja. Nothing of paddock's is stopped or moved.
- After the move, each moved project has a pull request landed through makiba.

## Definition of done

Checked by a person:

- Each moved project has had at least one pull request landed through makiba, and claude-org-ja has not been started in between.

## Notes

Outside the acceptance gate, and outside the schedule counted in plan.md section 9.
