# M5-6 makiba down and up keep the work

## Why

A resident organization is restarted for updates. Nothing in progress may be lost by that.

## What to build

- `makiba down`: record state, end worker terminals (the secretary only with `--secretary`, the base only with `--stop-base`), set runs `suspended`.
- `makiba up` after a down or a stop of the base: for each `suspended` run, launch again in its worktree with `resume=<conversation_id>`; if that launch is not verified, launch a fresh worker with the stored brief and the worktree as it is.
- On herdr-core, a kept shell left by a core stop is ended first.

## Carried from

- paddock: `skills/org-suspend`, `org-resume`, `org-down` (their rules, and Q4's decision)

## Definition of done

The first commit adds `tests/down_up.rs`, failing, with the trailer `Acceptance-Test: tests/down_up.rs`. At head `cargo test --test down_up` passes and asserts:

- After `down` then `up`, every run that was `in_use` is `in_use` again with one terminal.
- A run whose resume fails gets a fresh launch with the original brief.
- `down` never ends a terminal without the organization's tag.
