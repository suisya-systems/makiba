# M6-4 Pending decisions in the ledger

## Why

What waits for the person is the first thing stage 2 will notify about and stage 3 will answer from outside. It has to be a table, not a file one role owns.

## What to build

- `makiba pending add|escalate|withdraw|list` and `makiba answer <id> yes|no [--note]` for questions: the answer is queued as a message to the worker (`--typed` sends it typed, M2-4) and the entry becomes `answered`.
- The logic of `pending_decisions.py`, ported with the cases of its tests, with the store changed to the `pending` table.
- The `secretary_still` anomaly (M2-3) reads open entries.

## Carried from

- claude-org-ja: `tools/pending_decisions.py`, `tests/test_pending_decisions.py`

## Definition of done

The first commit adds `tests/pending.rs`, failing, with the trailer `Acceptance-Test: tests/pending.rs`. At head `cargo test --test pending` passes and asserts:

- The carried cases, ported, pass against the table.
- `answer` on an entry already answered is `conflict`.
- `answer` queues exactly one message to the run's worker.
