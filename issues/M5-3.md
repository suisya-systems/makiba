# M5-3 makibad owns the PR watches

## Why

A finished watcher that still looked alive (I-6), and a watch whose result nobody wrote (I-8).

## What to build

- `makiba pr opened|pushed`: records `pr_opened` / `fix_pushed`; inserts the `pr_watches` row for the new head and marks older rows for that PR `superseded`, in one transaction.
- `makiba/daemon/prwatch.py`: for each `watching` row, poll the checks of that exact head with `gh`; on a conclusion write `ci_completed` (with `head_oid`) and queue a notice; on an error it cannot recover from write `ci_watch_failed` and queue a notice. A row never stays `watching` after its poller is gone: on start every `watching` row gets a poller again.
- From the carried `pr_watch.py`: the check-reading and conclusion logic and their tests. Its own push to the secretary and its transport switch are removed.

## Carried from

- claude-org-ja: `tools/pr_watch.py`, `tools/test_pr_watch.py` (2,171 lines; the parts under `_notify*` and `_configured_transport` are not carried)

## Definition of done

The first commit adds `tests/test_pr_watch.py`, failing, with the trailer `Acceptance-Test: tests/test_pr_watch.py`. At head it passes and asserts:

- `pr pushed` with a new head leaves one `watching` row, for the new head.
- A watch whose `gh` call fails for good ends `failed` with a `ci_watch_failed` event and a notice.
- Restarting `makibad` with a `watching` row resumes polling it.
- `ci_completed` carries the head it was observed for.
