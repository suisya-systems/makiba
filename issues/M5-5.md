# M5-5 Run the triage scan and forward its result

## Why

Work discovery is what lets the organization propose its own next task. Its scan is deterministic and already tested; only its idea of free capacity was tied to a transport.

## What to build

- `src/work_discovery/`: the logic of claude-org-ja's `work_discovery_*.py`, ported. In the scan, capacity comes from `max_concurrent_workers` minus active runs, read through a function `makibad` supplies. The goal ledger keeps its format.
- After a close and at most once per 30 minutes, run the scan; if the candidate set differs from the last one sent, queue a notice to the secretary with the candidates.
- `work_discovery_scanned` as before.
