# M6-7 Language settings

## Why

Rules are English; the person need not be. And a project's pull requests follow the project.

## What to build

- Organization setting `conversation_language` (default: the environment's): one sentence from `roles/locale/` is appended to the secretary's launch.
- Project setting `artifact_language` (default `en`): one sentence appended to the worker's brief and passed to the pull-request and issue skills.
- `makiba config set|get` for both, person only.

## Carried from

- paddock: Q9's decision and the `conversation_language` handling in `org-start`

## Definition of done

The first commit adds `tests/language_settings.rs`, failing, with the trailer `Acceptance-Test: tests/language_settings.rs`. At head `cargo test --test language_settings` passes and asserts:

- With `conversation_language=ja` the secretary's launch arguments contain the Japanese sentence; with `en` they do not.
- A project without `artifact_language` gives a brief with the English sentence; with `ja`, the Japanese one.

## Notes

That a real secretary then answers in the set language is checked once on a host and recorded in `docs/runbooks/first-worker.md`; that part is outside the acceptance gate.
