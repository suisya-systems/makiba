# M7-2 Self-hosting, measured by landed pull requests and events met

## Why

The period is not measured in days. A week was chosen only because it is long enough to meet an update of the base, a secretary renewal and a failed launch at least once; at the present throughput (ten to fourteen pull requests a day) a count and the events themselves say more than seven days of calendar. This repeats the owner's earlier decision for the belt (`docs/design/belt.md`, Q4: decided after N = 10 stops from their record, not after days).

## What to build

- Develop makiba with makiba until both hold, with claude-org-ja not started in between: (1) twenty pull requests of makiba have landed through makiba; (2) each of three events has been met at least once: an update of the base, a renewal of the secretary, a failed launch.
- An event that has not happened by itself when the count is reached is brought about on purpose, and its handling recorded: an update of the base by a base version bump on the host, a renewal by the secretary's `renew` verb, a failed launch by a launch made to fail as the probes do.
- Every incident met: a row in `docs/design/incidents.md` and an Issue for its test.
- A short report in `docs/reports/self-hosting-week.md` (the file name is kept: nothing reads it by name, but a rename buys nothing and breaks the links already made to it): counts of runs, failed launches, undelivered messages at any point, renewals, time `makibad` was down; the count of landed pull requests; and for each of the three events, when it was met and whether it was brought about on purpose.

## Definition of done

Checked by a person:

- The report exists and shows the twenty landings and the three events.
- Every incident the report mentions has a row.
- The incident tests are green at the end.
