# M7-2 One week of self-hosting

## Why

A week is long enough to meet an update of the base, a secretary renewal and a failed launch at least once.

## What to build

- Develop makiba with makiba for seven days. claude-org-ja stays stopped.
- Every incident met: a row in `docs/design/incidents.md` and an Issue for its test.
- At the end, a short report in `docs/reports/self-hosting-week.md`: counts of runs, failed launches, undelivered messages at any point, renewals, time `makibad` was down.

## Definition of done

Checked by a person:

- The report exists and every incident it mentions has a row.
- The incident tests are green at the end.

## Notes

Outside the acceptance gate: closed by a person.

- G9 It runs its own development
